I have zero fitness or martial arts experience — can I join?

Yes. Beginners are welcome at any time. Every program is coached step by step, with a trainer for every session — you never train alone.

Which program is best for weight loss vs. self-defence?

For weight loss, the Fitness & Weight Loss program — fat-loss and weight-loss classes with special diet plans and charts, and a trainer for every session. For self-defence, Boxing, Kickboxing and MMA — practical self-defence, technique and body mechanics, taught step by step.

Do I have to spar, fight or compete?

No. Monthly competitions are for students enrolled in that sport. You can train for fitness, confidence and fat loss without competing — fight preparation is a separate path for amateurs and professionals who choose it.

For parents

Our goal is not just to teach kids to fight.

It is to help them become capable, confident young people who handle conflict with maturity, respect and good judgement. Children learn to stay calm, use their words, and get help — with physical skills taught as a last resort for safety.

Classes are structured, closely supervised and scaled to each child’s age and experience. And every week, guardians of minors get feedback from the coach on how their child is doing.

Is MMA and sparring safe for kids?

Classes are structured, closely supervised and scaled to each child’s age and experience, with a trainer for every session. Physical skills are taught as a last resort for safety — children learn to stay calm, use their words, and get help first. For sparring, protective gear must be worn, and it is provided by the academy before the session. A trainer is there for the whole session to oversee and conduct the spar.

How does weekly coach feedback work?

Guardians of minors get feedback from the coach every week. A glimpse of your child’s progress is also shared as a video in the community WhatsApp group.
